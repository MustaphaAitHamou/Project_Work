# Cookie policy

**Draft of 6 November 2026 — to be validated (ePrivacy Directive and national transpositions, PECR in the United Kingdom, Swiss FADP, Canadian laws). Text in square brackets must be completed or validated.**

## Principle

Tamurt Jobs uses **no advertising, audience-measurement or social-network cookies**. Only cookies and storage that are **strictly necessary** for the service, or for a feature you explicitly request, are used. They do not require prior consent. [TO VALIDATE: "strictly necessary" qualification of each tracker.]

If audience-measurement tools or other trackers were added, this policy would be updated and a consent mechanism would be put in place before they are activated.

## Trackers used

| Name | Type | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| `sb-<project>-auth-token` (possibly split into `.0`, `.1`…) | cookie, Tamurt Jobs | keep you signed in | until you sign out, at most 400 days |
| `NEXT_LOCALE` | cookie, Tamurt Jobs | remember the language you chose | 1 year |
| `theme` | browser local storage | remember the light or dark theme you chose | until you clear it |
| Cloudflare Turnstile | third-party script, only on protected forms when the check is enabled | tell humans from bots (security) | [TO CHECK with Cloudflare] |

Payment takes place on the secure **Stripe** page (stripe.com domain), which sets its own cookies, in particular for fraud prevention, under its own policy.

## Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking the session cookie prevents you from staying signed in; blocking the language cookie displays the default language.
