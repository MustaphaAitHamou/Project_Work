## Principle

Tamurt Jobs uses **no audience-measurement or social-network cookies**. Cookies and storage that are **strictly necessary** for the service, or for a feature you explicitly request, do not require prior consent. [TO VALIDATE: "strictly necessary" qualification of each tracker.]

**Advertising.** Public pages (home, job search, job pages, company pages) may show ads served by **Google AdSense**. Google and its partners only set or read advertising cookies and identifiers **with your consent**, collected by Google's consent management platform, certified and integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF). Without your consent, only non-personalised or limited ads may be shown. No ads appear in the candidate, recruiter and administration areas, nor on sign-in, payment and legal pages. Your account data, your CV and your applications are never shared with advertising networks. [TO VALIDATE: wording, list of partners shown by the consent platform, retention of choices.]

If audience-measurement tools or other trackers were added, this policy would be updated and a consent mechanism would be put in place before they are activated.

Payment takes place on the secure **Stripe** page (stripe.com domain), which sets its own cookies, in particular for fraud prevention, under its own policy.

## Managing cookies

You can change or withdraw your consent to advertising cookies at any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page. You can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings. To learn how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Blocking the session cookie prevents you from staying signed in; blocking the language cookie displays the default language.
