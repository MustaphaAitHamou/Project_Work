Before any publication, the company must be verified. Depending on the country, the Platform queries the available official registries and services (for example: Recherche d'entreprises/SIRENE, Companies House, VIES, Zefix) to check that the company exists and is active, that its legal name matches and, where the registry allows it, that the representative is listed. If these checks are inconclusive, a manual review of supporting documents is carried out.

No company is refused on the sole basis of an automated check: a refusal only occurs if the registry states that the company is not active, or after human review. The decision is sent by e-mail.

The representative guarantees that the information provided is accurate and that they are authorised to act on behalf of the company. ## 6. Content of offers and moderation

The company is solely responsible for the content of its offers, which must:

- describe a real, available job and comply with the employment law of the country of the position;
- contain **no discriminatory criterion** (origin, sex, age, family situation, religion, disability, sexual orientation, opinions, physical appearance, nationality where not required by law, etc.), except for a genuine and determining occupational or legal requirement;
- request **no payment** from candidates;
- allow candidates to apply **on Tamurt Jobs**, without redirecting them to another website, a messaging app or a telephone number to apply;
- comply with the language rules and mandatory information of the country concerned. [TO VALIDATE per country.]

Automated checks **flag** potentially discriminatory wording or requests to apply outside the Platform to a human moderator; they never reject an offer. Any change to a published offer may trigger a new review. The moderator may approve, refuse or suspend an offer; the decision is reasoned and notified by e-mail. A refusal or suspension stops the renewal of the subscription at the end of the current period.

Any visitor may report an offer. Reports are reviewed by the moderation team.

## 7. Failed payments

If a renewal fails, the company is informed by e-mail. The offer stays online during a **3-day grace period**, then is suspended until payment is settled; it is reactivated automatically as soon as Stripe confirms the payment.

## 8. Receiving applications and personal data

### 8.1 Limited access

The company **only** receives applications sent to its own offers: the profile information at the time of the application and the CV chosen by the candidate. It has no access to a CV database or to other candidates' profiles. CVs are downloaded through temporary links; each download is logged and visible to the candidate in their data export. The "viewed" status is shown to the candidate only when a member of the company has actually opened the application.

When a candidate withdraws their application or deletes their account, the company no longer has access to it on the Platform.

### 8.2 Role of the company

For the applications it receives, the company acts as an independent **controller** (or under the equivalent qualification of the applicable law). It undertakes to:

- use candidates' data only for the recruitment concerned;
- not disclose it to unauthorised third parties or reuse it for commercial purposes;
- inform candidates, keep the data for a limited period and answer the rights requests that concern it;
- secure downloaded CVs and delete them when they are no longer needed.

On the Platform, applications are anonymised **24 months** after the offer is closed (and at the latest 36 months after they were sent).
