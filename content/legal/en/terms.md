## 4. Services for candidates

### 4.1 Free of charge

Signing up, the profile, CV upload and applying are **free** for candidates. No paid offer is proposed to candidates.

### 4.2 Profile and CV

Candidates may upload CVs in PDF or DOCX format (4 MB maximum). Files are analysed before being stored; a file containing active content (macros, scripts) or that is encrypted is refused. CVs are stored in a private storage area and are never available at a permanent public address.

No photo is requested. Candidates are invited not to include in their CV data unrelated to the job sought (for example: origin, opinions, health, family situation, national identification number).

### 4.3 One-click application

Before the first application, candidates are informed of the data transmitted and give their agreement. For each application, the Platform transmits **only to the company that published the offer**: the profile information at the time of the application and the chosen CV.

A company never has access to all the profiles or CVs on the Platform: only to the applications received for its own offers.

### 4.4 Application tracking

Candidates see the status of each of their applications. The "viewed" status only appears once a member of the company has actually opened the application. Candidates may withdraw an application; the company then no longer has access to it.

### 4.5 No messaging

The Platform includes no messaging or chat system between candidates and recruiters. A recruiter who wishes to follow up contacts the candidate using the contact details provided in the application. Tamurt Jobs is not responsible for these exchanges.
