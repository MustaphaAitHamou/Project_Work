## 8. Security

Data is transmitted encrypted (HTTPS). CVs and documents are stored in private storage areas, only accessible through temporary links (60 seconds) issued after an authorisation check. Access to data is restricted by database-level rules, administration roles are separate, and sensitive actions are logged. Uploaded files are checked (actual type, active content, encryption). CVs are never sent as e-mail attachments.

## 9. Automated decisions

No decision producing legal effects or similarly significantly affecting you is made automatically: the Platform computes no score on candidates and rejects no application. Automated checks of offers only flag wording to a human moderator. The ranking of offers is explained on the [How ranking works](/en/how-ranking-works) page.
